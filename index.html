Altitude and temperature correction: site pressure P = 101,325 · (1 − 0.0065·h / 288.15)g·M / (R·L) (ISA), site density ρ = P·M / (R·T), correction factor = 1.225 / ρ. Net air at site = FAD × system efficiency ÷ correction factor, and BV = net air ÷ annular area. At sea level and 15 °C the factor is 1.000.

Conversions: 1 in = 25.4 mm · 1 m³/min = 35.3147 cfm · 1 m/s = 196.85 ft/min.
